Executive Summary

The Tri-Star Plan (TSP) is a strategic cybersecurity maturity roadmap utilized in public sector and enterprise environments—notably within Texas state agency compliance circles—to bridge the gap between technical controls and executive risk management. It categorizes security efforts into three distinct tiers: Hygiene, Resilience, and Optimization.

The strategic objective is to systematically transition capabilities from Star 1 (Manual/Reactive) to Star 3 (Automated/Strategic) by mapping technical execution to recognized GRC frameworks.
